02 / System architecture

One patient state.
Continuously revised evidence.

Patient history

Phenotype, HbA1c and baseline patterns

Observations

CGM readings, meals, wearable activity

State update

Time-aligned patient context and quality checks

Forecast

30 / 60 / 120-minute glucose scenarios

Clinician view

Trend, contributing context and availability

Safety path: unreliable or stale glucose → withhold prediction. Feedback path: a later measured glucose value is compared with the forecast issued earlier; the result becomes part of the audit record.
